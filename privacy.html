Privacy Policy — StarCoded Poster

Effective September 15, 2026.

StarCoded Poster is a private, single-operator tool used by the owner of the Star Coded Club accounts to publish the owner's own content to the owner's own TikTok and Instagram accounts. It is not offered to the public and has no users other than the account owner.

Data we access

Through the TikTok and Instagram APIs the tool accesses only the account owner's own profile identifiers and posting permissions, and uploads media created by the account owner. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any data about other people, followers, viewers, or third parties.

Storage

API tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer, in an access-restricted file, and are used solely to publish the owner's content. No data is transmitted to any third-party service other than TikTok, Instagram/Meta, and GitHub (which hosts the media files being published).

Deletion

The account owner can revoke the tool's access at any time from TikTok's or Instagram's connected-apps settings, and delete stored tokens by deleting the local credentials file.

Contact

Message @thestarcodedclub on TikTok or Instagram.
